About Me

My Journey

From operations to technology: a journey driven by curiosity, continuous growth, and a desire to solve real-world problems.

2013–2021

Where It All Began

My professional journey began informally at age 14 in a hardware store. In January 2017, at age 18, I was formally hired and remained with the company until August 2021.

2021

Operations and Leadership

In September 2021, I joined a window-screen company as a metalworker. Over time, my organizational, problem-solving, and leadership skills led to my promotion to Operations Manager, the role I hold today.

2022

First Steps in Programming

Alongside my work, I began studying programming independently in 2022 through free courses, YouTube videos, documentation, and personal projects.

2023

An Evolving Portfolio

In 2023, I built the first version of this portfolio. As I learned new technologies and concepts, I continued refining every part of it, turning it into a reflection of my growth as a developer.

2025

Data Science and Real-World Systems

In 2025, I began my Data Science degree at Universidade Santa Cecília, strengthening my theoretical and practical foundations in logic and algorithms, data modeling and analysis, cloud computing, relational and non-relational databases, statistics, and other core areas of technology.

Since starting my degree, I have also built two systems that help companies streamline their processes and create value through technology.

2026

The Next Chapter

Today, in 2026, my main goal is to complete my transition into the technology industry, focusing on Software Engineering. I aim to keep learning, take on increasingly complex challenges, and contribute to solutions that make a meaningful difference.
