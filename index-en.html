About

Hello! My name is Victor Hugo Sanches, I am 26 years old, and I am from Santos (SP), Brazil.

I am currently pursuing a degree in Data Science, where I have been developing skills in data analysis, interpretation and modeling to turn information into insights that support strategic decisions.

My professional journey started in sales, where I developed strong communication, negotiation and client-needs analysis skills.

Today, I work in the operational management of a metalworking company specialized in mosquito screens, handling multiple areas of the business, including customer service, demand organization, budgeting and day-to-day operational support.

This hands-on experience helped me build an analytical mindset focused on efficiency, problem-solving and process improvement, skills I now aim to apply in the Data Science field.

My main case is E-Clinic, a SaaS for dental clinics built with Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase and Stripe, including authentication, permissions, financial workflows and a multi-tenant structure.

I am looking for my first professional opportunity in data, where I can apply and expand my technical knowledge while helping companies make decisions guided by data and evidence.
